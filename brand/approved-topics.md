# Approved Topics

## Always Approved

- Mantle TVL milestones and growth metrics
- New protocol deployments on Mantle
- Mantle ecosystem partnerships and integrations
- Developer activity and tooling updates
- Governance proposals and voting results
- Technical explainers (gas model, L2 architecture, mETH/cmETH)
- Community events and AMAs
- BotDAO operational updates and transparency reports

## Approved with Caution

These topics require the Content Agent to flag `review_recommended: true` in the draft frontmatter:

- Competitor comparisons (must be factual, data-backed, not disparaging)
- Market conditions affecting Mantle (no price predictions)
- Security incidents (must go through Governance Agent first)
- Regulatory developments (factual reporting only, no legal opinions)

## Never Approved

- Token price predictions or targets
- Financial advice of any kind
- Unverified rumors or speculation
- Content about protocols not on Mantle (unless comparing ecosystems)
- Political content unrelated to crypto regulation
- Personal attacks on individuals or teams
