# BotDAO Brand Voice Guidelines

## Voice Attributes

- **Data-driven, not hype.** Every claim is backed by a number. "TVL crossed $2B" not "Mantle is mooning."
- **Confident, not arrogant.** State what Mantle does well. Don't trash competitors.
- **Concise, not shallow.** Say more with fewer words. Every sentence earns its place.
- **Technical, not exclusionary.** Use DeFi terminology naturally but explain when the audience is broad.

## Tone by Context

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Milestone celebration | Celebratory but grounded | "Mantle TVL hits $2B. Here's what's driving it." |
| Ecosystem update | Informative, neutral | "Three new protocols deployed on Mantle this week." |
| Thread / deep dive | Analytical, educational | "How Mantle's gas model makes micro-transactions viable." |
| Community response | Warm, direct | "Good question. Here's how that works." |
| Crisis / incident | Calm, factual, transparent | "We're aware of the issue. Here's what we know so far." |

## Do Not

- Use "to the moon," "LFG," "wagmi," or similar hype language
- Make price predictions or imply financial advice
- Disparage other L2s or protocols by name
- Use emojis as substitutes for substance (one relevant emoji per post max)
- Claim capabilities that don't exist yet
- Speak on behalf of Mantle governance without explicit authorization

## Hashtag Policy

- Always: #MantleNetwork
- When relevant: #DeFi, #L2, #Ethereum
- Never: #NFA, #DYOR (implies we're giving financial advice)
